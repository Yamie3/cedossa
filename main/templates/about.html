About CEDOSSA

Centre for Down Syndrome, Sickle Cell and Autism — a Malawian nonprofit empowering persons who are differently able through advocacy, skills development and psychosocial support.

Our Story

How CEDOSSA Began

The Centre for Down Syndrome, Sickle Cell and Autism (CEDOSSA) was established in 2021 and registered as a nonprofit organisation in 2022.

It was founded after our founder lost her child who had Down syndrome. Out of that loss grew a determination to ensure persons living with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism are accepted, supported, and able to enjoy their rights like anyone else in Malawi.

Our Mission

To empower persons with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism to enjoy their rights through advocacy, skills development, and psychosocial counselling and trainings.

Our Vision

To ensure persons with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism are accepted and enjoying their rights like anyone else in Malawi.

Our Objectives

The goals that guide everything we do.

1

To provide a Centre for acquiring skills for independence and therapies for persons with Down syndrome and autism.

2

To provide counselling services and trainings on Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism management.

3

To advocate for policy formulation, review and implementation in response to the human rights of persons with disabilities.

4

To influence positive attitudes in the community towards people with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism.

5

To work in cooperation with Government, churches and other non-governmental organisations that pursue similar objectives and aims.
