About CEDOSSA

Centre for Down Syndrome, Sickle Cell, and Autism — a Malawian non-governmental organisation dedicated to care, education, advocacy, and community engagement.

Our Mission

To empower children living with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease, and autism, together with their families, through inclusive healthcare, developmental learning, early intervention, and compassionate community support.

Our Vision

A Malawi where every child, regardless of ability, is embraced, supported, and given the opportunity to thrive within a caring and inclusive society.

What We Do

Autism Support

Early intervention, sensory activities, speech therapy, and inclusive education.

Down Syndrome Support

Specialized healthcare, developmental learning, and advocacy programs.

Sickle Cell Support

Medical support, awareness, and community education for affected families.

Your support helps us reach more children and families across Malawi.
