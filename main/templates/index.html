About Us

Who We Are

The Centre for Down Syndrome, Sickle Cell and Autism (CEDOSSA) was established in 2021 and registered as a nonprofit organisation in 2022.

It was founded after our founder lost her child who had Down syndrome — turning a deeply personal loss into a movement so that persons who are differently able are accepted and enjoy their rights like anyone else in Malawi.

Our Purpose

We see ability, not limitation. Every person living with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease or autism is worthy of acceptance, dignity and the full enjoyment of their rights.

Our Mission

To empower persons with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism through advocacy, skills development, and psychosocial counselling and trainings.

Our Vision

That persons with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism are accepted and enjoy their rights like anyone else in Malawi.

Differently Able

We champion a Malawi where being differently able is met with acceptance, inclusion and equal opportunity — never stigma.

Our Objectives

What we set out to achieve for persons with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism in Malawi.

1

To provide a Centre for acquiring skills for independence and therapies for persons with Down syndrome and autism.

2

To provide counselling services and trainings on Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism management.

3

To advocate for policy formulation, review and implementation in response to the human rights of persons with disabilities.

4

To influence positive attitudes in the community towards people with Down syndrome, sickle cell disease and autism.

5

To work in cooperation with Government, churches and other non-governmental organisations that pursue similar objectives and aims.
