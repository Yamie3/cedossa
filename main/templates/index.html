1 in 18 children in Malawi live with a disability

The reality for children in Malawi

  • Nearly 2 out of 3 children grow up in poverty, with limited access to nutrition, healthcare and education.
  • 2 out of 5 risk developmental delays before the age of five.
  • Most children with a disability are out of school and left without the care they need.

Who We Are

Our Belief
